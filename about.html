About Us

Built by coaches, for coaches.

Remedy Coaching is the professional network for sports coaches: coaches build verified profiles and get hired for paid consulting work, from film review to full program builds.

Every year, thousands of athletes finish their playing careers with years of knowledge about technique, preparation and the game. Most have no structured way to share it, so they coach informally or leave the sport altogether. Meanwhile, athletes, schools, clubs and programs need exactly that expertise and struggle to find it.

Remedy Coaching connects the two. On our platform, coaches and former athletes build a professional profile that shows their playing and coaching record, certifications and peer endorsements. We cover 13 sports: football, basketball, baseball, soccer, wrestling, track and field, volleyball, lacrosse, tennis, pickleball, swimming, hockey, and strength and conditioning. Coaches in any other sport can join too. Athletes and organizations hire them for one-on-one coaching sessions, film review and full program builds. Coaches build a reputation that brings more work.

Remedy Coaching is built for coaches, by coaches. The platform is live and we're now bringing on our first coaches and organizations.
